# Attribution and scope

## Icon source

Five category icons are adaptations of Tabler Icons by Paweł Kuna and contributors:
`plane`, `ship`, `microphone`, `map-pin`, `access-point`.
The full upstream MIT notice is in `TABLER-MIT.txt`. Keep that notice when distributing
these icons, their derivatives, the SVG sprite, or the React icon component.
The aviation-data, paging and mesh pictograms are custom additions created for this
WaveKit pack on the same grid and stroke system. They are not upstream Tabler icons.
See `docs/ICONOGRAPHY.md` for a per-icon provenance record and the exact modifications.

## Lettering and fonts

The signal-w is carried over from the supplied WaveKit vector master. The integrated
lettering uses Inter Display SemiBold, with custom spacing and positioning. The fixed
baseline uses Noto Sans Condensed Bold. Both are converted to drawing paths, not
embedded as font software. Inter and Noto are credited as source typeface families,
not as extra webfont requirements. Noto font metadata: Copyright 2015 Google LLC;
Inter: Rasmus Andersson and contributors.

Live editorial typography is specified as D-DIN Condensed (Datto / Charles Nix,
Monotype) and Noto Sans, following the owner's supplied R48 typography specification.
The live fonts are distributed in `fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, each
with its `OFL.txt`: D-DIN Condensed (Copyright 2017 Datto Inc., Reserved Font Name
"D-DIN Condensed"; upstream WOFF2, unmodified) and Noto Sans (Copyright 2022 The Noto
Project Authors; Latin subset). They are not covered by the Tabler MIT notice.

SIL's OFL guidance permits use of OFL fonts in logos and graphics; a vector logo is
not a redistribution of the font family. Source references and font setup are in
`docs/SOURCES.md` and `fonts/README.md`. When adding further font software, retain
its actual license and copyright notice; do not infer permissions from this file.
