# The identity

## Brand idea

A continuous signal trace becomes the letter w. The waveform is the first letter of
the integrated wordmark, not a decorative badge next to an ordinary first w.

The visual character is precise, calm and readable: flat colour, generous space,
consistent outlines, a limited palette and restrained spectrum imagery. This is a
software-defined-radio identity, not an ocean, music, surfing or Wi-Fi identity.

## Naming and baseline

Name in prose: **WaveKit**. Logo artwork: **wavekit**.

Official baseline: **MAKE SENSE OF THE SPECTRUM**.

Use the baseline on covers, repository hero banners, social cards and introductory
surfaces. Omit it from narrow navigation bars, favicons, app icons and compact UI.
It is official even when not displayed. Do not translate or rewrite it without approval.
The editorial cover shows the same sentence on two lines; that is a layout change,
not a second slogan.

Descriptor when useful: **Software-defined radio**. Avoid additional generic claims,
marketing triples, invented catchphrases and unverified “open source” or license badges.
The project repository is Coriou/WaveKit; no public marketing domain is asserted.

## Visual vocabulary

The core elements are the integrated wordmark, signal mark, category pictograms,
flat ink/paper fields, restrained mint or forest accents, and the unlabelled spectrum.
Use the spectrum as secondary artwork. It is never the logo and never evidence of
actual decoder performance. Do not add gradients, shadows or glow to the logo.

Pale phosphor belongs on a dark field. Forest is its high-contrast counterpart for
light surfaces. Keep icon labels and paragraph text in a readable foreground colour.

## Authority of the deliverable

This pack resolves the previous concept boards' conflicting colours, fonts, names,
minimum sizes, protocol groupings and invented domain. Use the supplied asset names
and documented type roles instead of copying literal details from generated images.
A future public brand-use license and any trademark clearance remain owner decisions.
