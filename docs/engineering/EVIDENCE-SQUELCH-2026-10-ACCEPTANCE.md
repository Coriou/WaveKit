# Evidence squelch: acceptance (October 2026)

Requirements: [evidence-squelch](../specs/evidence-squelch/requirements.md) 8.1–8.4.
Plans: [design, Fake-link acceptance and Live acceptance plan](../specs/evidence-squelch/design.md).

## Purpose

Records the acceptance of the evidence auto squelch: the fake-link scenario (requirement 8.2,
plan task M.2) on the default csdr engine and on Rust at 2.048 and 2.4 Msps, plus a CPU-quota
variant; the checkpoint 8.4 decision for AM (task M.3); and the live run with the user
(requirement 8.3, task M.4). Failures become amendments in the spec.

The last row is the syllable-timed audio-frequency FSK burst case (about 100 ms on, 100 ms off, or
500 ms on, 250 ms off) from the known limitation after requirement 1.4; it is not a pass or fail
criterion. The marginal-level voice row is the known limitation after requirement 8.1; it runs only
in the unit end-to-end tests, not on the fake link.

`am-carrier` and `nfm-clicks` carry `rollingWindowRisk` in `manifest.json`: some rolling 20-frame
windows sit near the voice thresholds. Record whether they open.

Verdicts per fixture (requirement 8.2): opened or not; first played sample within 20 ms of the
over's onset; delay per over at most 1.5 s; stream byte rate within 0.5 % of nominal per minute;
transmission events equal to a manual-mode run; event-loop p99 within 2 ms of manual mode; close
guard covers the measured back-chain drain.
