Components

Every recipe of the UI kit in every size, variant and state, in three scopes side by side: True black + Mono (the default), Ink + phosphor and Paper + Mono. Under each recipe, the dashed "Today" strip shows what the boards draw now and what the recipe replaces. Hover and click are live; focus is shown forced. Overrule any pick here before the boards migrate.
